Myth vs. fact

Owning may be closer than you think

"I need 20% down."

FHA loans start at 3.5% down, and some conventional loans start at 3% for qualified buyers.

"My credit has to be perfect."

Many loan programs work with less-than-perfect credit. You can check your reports free every week at AnnualCreditReport.com, and our lender partner can tell you what to work on.

"I need a lot of savings."

Colorado has down payment assistance programs, like CHFA and Metro DPA, for buyers who qualify.
