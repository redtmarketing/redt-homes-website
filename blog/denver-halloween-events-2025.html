🎃👻 Spooky Season Finale: Denver’s Ultimate Halloween Events – October 31, 2025

← Back to Blog

By Kevin B · September 17, 2025 · Uncategorized

As the witching hour approaches, Denver Halloween events bring thrills, chills, and family-friendly fun. Whether you’re seeking haunted houses in Denver, pumpkin patches, or festive fall activities, the Mile High City has something for everyone this Halloween.

👻 Haunted Attractions & Spooky Fun

  • 13th Floor Haunted House – One of the scariest haunted houses in Denver, featuring attractions like "Legend of the 13th Floor" and "The Blood Shed." Perfect for thrill-seekers.
  • Paranormal Palace – Day of the Dead – Immerse yourself in a themed Halloween experience in Denver with live entertainment and a vibrant Day of the Dead atmosphere.
  • Boo at the Zoo – A family favorite for Halloween activities in Denver, offering trick-or-treating, seasonal snacks, and encounters with cryptid creatures at the Denver Zoo.
    •  

🎃 Pumpkin Patches & Fall Festivities

  • Chatfield Farms Pumpkin Festival – Celebrate fall at this beloved festival with a 10-acre pumpkin patch near Denver, corn maze, and family-friendly activities.
  • Mile High Farms – Explore Colorado’s best pumpkin patch and corn maze, perfect for families and fall outings.
  • Four Mile Historic Park Pumpkin Harvest Festival – Enjoy crafting, games, and pumpkin picking at this historic park.
  • Maize in the City – Family-friendly Denver pumpkin patch featuring a corn maze, petting zoo, and a variety of pumpkins.
  • Anderson Farms – U-pick pumpkins, giant corn maze, and fun fall activities for all ages near Denver.

🍂 Family-Friendly Halloween Events

After a day of fall adventures, cozy up at one of Denver’s seasonal dining spots:

  • Magic of the Jack O’ Lanterns – Stroll through a glowing trail of over 7,000 hand-carved pumpkins, including dinosaurs, pirate ships, and dragons.
  • Treat Street at Arapahoe County Scaregrounds – Safe and fun trick-or-treating experience for all ages.

🍽️ Halloween-Themed Dining & Nightlife

  • Halloween Bar Crawls – Join the biggest Halloween events in Denver with multiple bars offering drink specials, costume contests, and nightlife fun.
  • Spooky Dining Experiences – Many local restaurants and breweries offer Halloween-themed menus and events. Check local listings for special offerings on October 31.

From heart-pounding haunted houses to charming pumpkin patches and festive bar crawls, Denver offers a plethora of Halloween activities for families and adults. Whether you’re seeking thrills, family fun, or seasonal delights, the Mile High City has something for everyone this spooky season.
