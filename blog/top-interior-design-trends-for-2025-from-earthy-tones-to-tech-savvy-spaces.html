Top Interior Design Trends for 2025: From Earthy Tones to Tech-Savvy Spaces

Design in 2025 is about much more than aesthetics—it’s about creating spaces that elevate your lifestyle, promote well-being, and connect you to your environment in intentional ways. At redT Homes, we’re proud to lead the way in design-forward construction, blending timeless craftsmanship with modern functionality.

Whether you’re building from the ground up or personalizing your finishes, these are the top interior design trends shaping how we live, gather, and recharge in 2025.

1. Earthy, Rooted Color Palettes

The natural world is influencing everything—from exterior finishes to kitchen backsplashes. Expect to see muted greens, clay terracotta, warm browns, and sandstone neutrals dominating color selections this year. These tones bring calm, comfort, and depth into the home, creating a retreat-like atmosphere.

💡 redT Insight: We’re seeing more buyers lean into two-tone cabinetry with matte black hardware, warm wood accents, and mineral-inspired tile to complement these grounded hues.

3. Statement Lighting as Architectural Art

Lighting isn’t just a necessity—it’s a statement. Think sculptural fixtures, natural materials like rattan or hand-blown glass, and layered lighting plans that enhance mood, depth, and texture.

✨ What’s New: Oversized pendant lights over kitchen islands and art-inspired sconces in stairwells are among the top requests in our design studio this year.

4. Tech-Integrated Living That Feels Seamless

Smart home tech has matured—and in 2025, it’s less about gadgets and more about creating a connected, intuitive home experience. From voice-activated lighting to whole-home air purification systems, functionality and health-forward features go hand in hand.

5. Texture-Rich, Eco-Conscious Materials

Expect to see a rise in plaster walls, natural wood finishes, stone surfaces, and limewash paints—all adding depth and dimension while aligning with eco-conscious values. More buyers are asking, “What’s this made of?”, and we’re here for it.

🌱 Sustainably Designed: redT prioritizes materials that are low-VOC, long-lasting, and locally sourced whenever possible—because what surrounds you, matters.

6. Spa-Like Primary Suites with a Focus on Wellness

The bathroom is no longer an afterthought. Today’s primary suites are designed for relaxation and rejuvenation, with freestanding tubs, oversized showers, integrated lighting, and tactile finishes that evoke a high-end spa.

🛁 Design Detail: Heated floors, calming neutral tiles, and smart mirrors are all gaining traction with our buyers in 2025.

Final Thoughts: Style Meets Substance at redT

Interior design in 2025 is defined by connection—to nature, to technology, and to personal well-being. At redT, our homes reflect this evolution by offering curated finishes, smart floorplans, and the flexibility to make your home truly yours.

Whether you're starting fresh with a new build or exploring options in one of our curated collections, our design-forward process ensures your home is on-trend, livable, and built for the way you want to live.
