What Is Going on In The DTC?

← Back to Blog

By redT Homes Marketing · January 29, 2020 · Home Buyers Insights

[caption id="attachment_50" align="alignnone" width="225"] exc-5e065f60b75a7066e35980a3[/caption]

When you buy a home, you buy a lifestyle. The Denver Tech Center life is a busy one, but a fun one that gives you access to nearly anything you want to do all within a reasonable distance of your home. There are many high-end retail centers that surround the DTC with exclusive shops and dining options.

redT’s exclusive listing, The Flats at VillaRosso is ideally located minutes from the center of the DTC and seconds from the Shops & Restaurants at Belleview Station. Living at the Flats at VillaRosso gives you access to the amenities of Greenwood Village, DTC, Belleview Station, and The Landmark, and fun local events like Art in the Park, and Greenwood Village Day. It’s the best of urban and suburban living.

The Shops at Belleview Station is a short 5-minute stroll from your front door and includes a handful of dining options and nightlife fun. Whether you’re wanting a quick cup of coffee, a cute date night, or a fine dining meal, you can find it all at the Shops at Belleview Station. The shops also offer limited boutique options like A-Line Boutique and Omega Seed Spice and physical fitness programs such as OrangeTheory Fitness and Barre3.

Having a balanced lifestyle is what can bring peace and comfort of the home, out of the home. From local coffee roasters to signature happy hour hotspots to fun weekend outings - here are the rest of our top picks from the DTC area:

Top Picks at Belleview Station:

Corvus Coffee Roasters

Right in the middle of it all is your favorite coffee destination- Corvus Coffee. With a cute patio and modern interior, it will soon be your favorite shop to get your morning pick me up.

 

Sushi-Rama

A very unique and transforming sushi experience that is unlike any other you may have previously experienced. Sushi is delivered on a conveyor belt that extends throughout the restaurant, bringing sushi rolls in smaller sizes so you have the freedom to try many rolls as you please at an affordable price.

Los Chingones

Enjoy $5 happy hour items on the patio with friends or take your pick of the endless tequila options to make your own margarita! This destination is ideal for any happy hour meet-ups or a place to hit on your way home from work. You will be sure to love so much around your new home.

Ruth’s Chris

If the time ever comes and you need to rise to the occasion, take a shot at Ruth’s Chris Steakhouse. This fine dining restaurant is located in Belleview Station and offers an upscale menu that is sure to impress; whether it is date night or time to take the boss out, you will be sure to impress.

Top Picks at The Landmark 

Comedy Works

Catch dinner and a movie at Comedy Works! They are teamed up with neighboring restaurants Lucy and the Lila B Lounge, so going for dinner and a movie or movies and a drink is totally attainable and nearby.

Zeal

For all of our food enthusiasts… This place is for you! The freshest ingredients are brought to the restaurant for everyone to enjoy. The place for weekend brunch, happy hour, and a light meal stop on by at Zeal because you are sure to find something you love.

Pizza Republica

For a quick, authentic bite to eat Pizza Republica is a fun place to be. With only 2 locations in the Denver area, this spot is always the craze. Whether you want to hit somewhere for early happy hour from 2:30-6:30 or call it in to-go this will check all the boxes.

 

Upgrade to DTC Living

If you want to consider a new way of living in the Denver Tech Center, consider your start at The Flats at VillaRosso. This condo community is made up of 1 and 2 bedroom residences with high-end finishes and expansive outdoor spaces that boast a bright and open living concept.
