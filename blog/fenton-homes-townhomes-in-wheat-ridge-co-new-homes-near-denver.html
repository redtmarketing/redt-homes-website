Fenton Homes & Townhomes in Wheat Ridge, CO: Modern Living Near Denver

If you are looking for new homes in Wheat Ridge, CO or modern townhomes near Denver, Fenton by redT Homes is a community worth watching. Located in East Wheat Ridge, just 6 miles from Downtown Denver, Fenton is designed for buyers who want contemporary design, functional layouts, and a connected location close to parks, dining, shopping, and recreation.

Fenton introduces a fresh option for homebuyers who want the convenience of Denver access without giving up the neighborhood feel of Wheat Ridge. redT Homes describes the community as a modern residential neighborhood that combines small-town charm with city convenience, making it appealing for buyers seeking both comfort and connectivity.

What Is Fenton in Wheat Ridge?

Fenton is a new home community in East Wheat Ridge, Colorado, featuring two residential formats: single-family homes and townhome-style homes. According to the property page, both are designed with contemporary layouts, open living areas, and practical floor plans intended for everyday comfort and entertaining.

For buyers comparing new construction options in the Denver metro area, that mix matters. Fenton offers a more design-forward alternative for people who want a newly built home with modern flow, usable indoor-outdoor space, and attached garage parking.

Where Is Fenton Located?

Fenton is located at 3780 Fenton St., Wheat Ridge, CO 80212. Approximately 6 miles from Downtown Denver, giving residents convenient access to the city while remaining close to local parks, trails, restaurants, and shopping in Wheat Ridge and the surrounding area.

That location is a major part of the appeal. Buyers searching for homes near Downtown Denver, Wheat Ridge new construction, or townhomes with easy city access often want three things: commute convenience, neighborhood character, and nearby recreation. Fenton is positioned to speak directly to that demand because the community emphasizes all three.

Home Features at Fenton

Homes at Fenton include: approximately 1,500 to 1,700 square feet, 2 to 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, private rooftop decks, attached two-car garages, and open living and kitchen spaces.

These details make the community attractive to a wide range of buyers, including first-time buyers moving up from a condo, professionals who want extra flexibility for a home office, and downsizers who still want a modern layout and low-fuss living.

Why Fenton Stands Out in the Wheat Ridge Market

Fenton is positioned around modern comfort and convenience. The contemporary home design, efficient living, stylish finishes, functional floor plans, and a location near both recreation and Denver amenities.

From a buyer’s perspective, that creates a compelling value proposition:

  • Modern single-family and townhome options in one community
  • Rooftop decks that extend living space
  • Attached two-car garages for convenience
  • Open layouts built for everyday living and entertaining
  • A Wheat Ridge address close to Denver, parks, dining, and shopping

Who Should Consider Fenton?

Fenton may be a strong fit for buyers who want:

  • A newly built home in Wheat Ridge
  • Easy access to Downtown Denver
  • Modern floor plans with open living areas
  • A private rooftop deck
  • A single-family home or townhome-style option in a connected neighborhood setting

Because the community offers both single-family and townhome-style homes, it can appeal to buyers with different lifestyle needs while keeping a consistent modern design language across the project.
