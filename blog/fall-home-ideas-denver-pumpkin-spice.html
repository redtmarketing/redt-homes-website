Pumpkin Spice & Everything Nice: Making Your Denver Home Feel Like Fall

 

There’s something magical about fall in Denver. The crisp mountain air, golden aspen leaves, and cozy nights make this season one of the best times to truly enjoy your home. At redT Homes, we believe fall is more than just a season — it’s a chance to create warmth, comfort, and sustainability in the spaces where you live.

Here are a few simple ways to welcome fall into your home and make it smell, feel, and look like the season we all love.

Fill Your Home with Natural Fall Scents

Instead of synthetic candles or sprays, bring in the cozy aromas of the season with natural ingredients. Simmer a small pot of water on the stove with:

  • Orange peels 🍊

  • A few cinnamon sticks

  • Whole cloves

  • Star anise

Within minutes, your home will be filled with a warm, inviting scent — all without harsh chemicals.

🎃 DIY Organic Pumpkin Spice Mix

Pumpkin spice doesn’t just belong in lattes. Whip up your own organic blend to use in baking, oatmeal, or even sprinkled on roasted squash. Here’s a simple recipe:

  • 3 tbsp ground organic cinnamon

  • 2 tsp ground organic ginger

  • 2 tsp ground organic nutmeg

  • 1 tsp ground organic allspice

  • 1 tsp ground organic cloves

Mix and store in a sealed glass jar. This little spice jar is autumn magic — add it to coffee, warm milk, or baked goods for instant fall flavor.

Cozy Up Your Living Spaces

Fall is the season of layering. Add warm throws, textured pillows, and earth-toned decor to your living room or bedroom. For a sustainable touch, consider choosing organic cotton or recycled fabric options — better for the planet and your home.

🔥 Create an Outdoor Retreat

Denver’s fall evenings are perfect for fire pits, string lights, and cozy blankets under the stars. Whether you have a patio, rooftop, or backyard, a little seasonal decorating goes a long way. Sustainable outdoor furniture made from reclaimed or FSC-certified wood is a great way to make your outdoor space both stylish and eco-friendly.

Why redT Homes Loves Fall

At redT Homes, we design spaces that feel as good as they look. From energy-efficient construction to eco-friendly finishes, our homes are built to keep you comfortable year-round. Just like a cozy fall evening, your home should be a place where warmth, sustainability, and style come together.

🍂 Fall in Denver is short but oh-so-sweet — why not make the most of it by filling your home with cozy vibes, natural scents, and sustainable touches? Whether you’re sipping cider by the fire pit or baking pumpkin bread in your kitchen, your home should be your favorite place to enjoy the season.
