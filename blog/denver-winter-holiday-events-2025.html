❄️ Celebrate the Season: Denver’s 2025 Winter Events & Festivities

As the crisp Colorado air settles in, Denver transforms into a holiday wonderland filled with twinkling lights, festive markets, and joyful celebrations. Whether you're a longtime resident or new to the area, here’s your guide to the must-see winter events around the Mile High City this December.

🎄 1. Denver Christkindlmarket

  • When: November 21 – December 23, 2025

  • Where: Tivoli Quad, Auraria Campus

  • Why Go: Experience an authentic German-style holiday market featuring local and European craft vendors, festive foods, and live entertainment. It’s free, family-friendly, and even dog-friendly

🎅 2. 9NEWS Parade of Lights

  • When: December 6, 2025, at 6 p.m.

  • Where: Downtown Denver

  • Why Go: This beloved tradition features over 40 illuminated floats, giant balloons, marching bands, and a special appearance by Santa and Major Waddles the Penguin

🛍️ 3. Cherry Creek Holiday Market

  • When: November 20 – December 24, 2025

  • Where: Fillmore Plaza, Cherry Creek North

  • Why Go: Shop from over 50 local artisan vendors, enjoy live music, and indulge in festive treats. The market is free, open to the public, and family- and dog-friendly

⛸️ 4. McGregor Skate

  • When: November 28, 2025 – January 4, 2026

  • Where: McGregor Square, LoDo

  • Why Go: Enjoy ice skating in the heart of Denver’s vibrant LoDo district. Skate rentals are available on-site, making it a convenient and fun activity for all ages

🌟 5. Grand Illumination at Denver Union Station

  • When: November 24, 2025

  • Where: Denver Union Station

  • Why Go: Witness the magical lighting of the 40-foot-tall Christmas tree and enjoy live music, festive treats, and visits from Santa and Mrs. Claus

🎁 Pro Tips for Enjoying Denver’s Winter Festivities

  • Family-Friendly Fun: Events like the Parade of Lights and Christkindlmarket are perfect for all ages.

  • Date Night Delight: Enjoy a romantic evening at McGregor Skate or the Grand Illumination.

  • Shop Local: Support local artisans at the Cherry Creek Holiday Market and Christkindlmarket.

  • Stay Warm: Dress in layers and enjoy hot cocoa or mulled wine at the events
