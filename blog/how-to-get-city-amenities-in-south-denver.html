How to Get City Amenities in South Denver

← Back to Blog

By redT Homes Marketing · June 5, 2020 · Home Buyers Insights

Many benefits that come with moving into the city— many being the nearby amenities that are walking or biking distance from your door. However, drive 20-minutes south of downtown and you will find yourself in the Denver Tech Center (DTC) which will satisfy any city needs. We have covered the history of the DTC in our previous blog post as well as the great things that are going on in various parts of the DTC! In case you forgot, we will post the links below!

Previous Blogs: History of the Denver Tech Center What is Going On In the DTC?

If you are looking for a neighborhood that embodies the qualities of both urban and suburban life, the Denver Tech Center might be the right neighborhood for you. Get urban amenities and a suburban neighborhood that is away from all the loud hustle and bustle of the city. A condo community in the Denver Tech Center, the Flats at VillaRosso can give you everything in a suburban neighborhood and just as much with great urban amenities.

The Flats at VillaRosso is located on Monaco Street and a 5-minute walk away from the shops and restaurants at Belleview Station. The location of the Flats is unmatched— there are no commercial developments on the West side of the building, allowing for great front range views and Monaco Park and Open Space is a 4-minute walk from the building. The condo retreat promotes luxury and location and is perfect for any buyer looking to find that a balance of city and suburban life. If you can’t decide, meet yourself in the middle at the Denver Tech Center! The amenities that you have walking and biking distance while living at the Flats are on par with anything you can find in the city. From a fenced dog park to a light rail station to local retailers, the DTC has it all within walking or biking distance the Flats at VillaRosso.

Great amenities in the DTC include access to the Belleview Station Dog Park, the Belleview Station Light Rail stop, walkable fitness centers, local boutique shopping, classic and fine dining options, as well as a Comedy Works as a perfect form of entertainment. Discover a new side of Denver without a grid of streets and a side that has numerous pedestrian and bike paths seconds from your front door.

There is much to do at the Tech Center, come discover what it means to live a sophisticated metro life at The Flats at VillaRosso. If you are still curious to find out more, please reach out to our sales team at 720-402-4385.

City Amenities, Suburban-(ish) Neighborhood

If you want to consider a new way of living in the Denver Tech Center, consider your start at The Flats at VillaRosso. This condo community is made up of 1 and 2 bedroom residences with high-end finishes and expansive outdoor spaces that boast bright and open living concepts.
