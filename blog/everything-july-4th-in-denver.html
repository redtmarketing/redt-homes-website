Everything July 4th in Denver

← Back to Blog

By redT Homes Marketing · July 3, 2020 · Home Buyers Insights

There is always a lot going on in Denver on summer weekends, especially on the weekend of July 4th! This weekend in Denver, there may be a lack of large firework shows but there is no shortage of other fun to be had. Whether you want to drive out of town to see firework displays, take a First Friday Art Walk, or visit any outdoor festivals we have got a lot of recommendations on where to start off your fun weekend! Fireworks in Denver have been put on pause due to the COVID-19 pandemic and the tight rules against large gatherings in stadiums or other similar venues, as well as drought warnings throughout many parts of Denver. However, there are still a handful of towns near Denver that are able to throw their fireworks show as planned! Cozy up in your car somewhere close or find a nearby park where you can admire the fireworks go off in the sky. Castle Rock Fireworks 2156 Red Hawk Ridge Dr, Castle Rock, CO 80109 Show starts at 9:30 PM Morrison Fireworks at Bandimere Speedway 3051 S Rooney Rd, Morrison, CO 80465 July 4th Festivities start at 3 PM, Fireworks start at 9:30 PM Brighton Fireworks 650 E Southern St, Brighton, CO 80601 Show starts at dusk  
 

Other events that are being put on this July 4th weekend is the Celebration of American Diversity Festival, the First Friday Art Walk in Five Points, and the Artists on Sante Fe Gallery and Art Walk! All these events are being held outdoors and in compliance with all Covid-19 regulations in the City of Denver.

Celebration of American Diversity Festival July 3rd + 4th from 5:00 PM to 9:30 PM Stanley Marketplace - Westfield - 2501 Dallas St., Aurora, CO 80010 Price: Kids 0 - 3 FREE, Kids 3 - 18 start at $15, Adults start at $25 Stanley Marketplace will have private 10’ X 10’ Picnic Areas for guests to enjoy live music, entertainments, and dinner + drinks!

First Friday Art Walk on Sante Fe

Get out there and enjoy the 4th of July weekend! Whether you are finding a place to hunker down for fireworks, a place to roam on a Friday night, or looking for a day full of art and music, we are sure that you will have an amazing weekend! Have fun & be safe.
