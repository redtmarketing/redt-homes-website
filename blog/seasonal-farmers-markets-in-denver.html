Seasonal Farmers’ Markets in Denver

← Back to Blog

By redT Homes Marketing · July 17, 2020 · Home Buyers Insights

The Mile High City is well versed in the love for naturally cultivated goods from farm-fresh produce to tasty baked treats. There is more to Denver’s Farmers’ Markets than just fresh produce— local artists, craftsmen, and musicians all line the streets with cultivators to put on spectacular, friendly markets on a weekly basis that tie in the community in ways you have never seen.

Each farmers’ market takes on its own unique twist to make their market ideal for the community they are serving. A festive weekly get-together boasting local art, produce, live music and entertainment, and a beautiful crowd of your local neighbors. Enjoy this time of seasonal farmers’ markets in Denver. We would love to share our favorite Denver Farmers’ Markets that are held in neighborhoods where redT has a variety of homes for sale or for rent! Check all the local farmers’ markets by neighborhood below!

Denver Tech Center

People and Produce at Belleview Station

WHERE: 4910 S Newport St., Denver, CO 80237 WHEN: Every Sunday 9AM to 1PM from June 14th to September 27th THINGS TO KNOW: Masks are required and you are allowed 2 people per party. Unfortunately, dogs are not allowed at this farmers’ market. Curbside pickup is also an option, order online here.

University Park

South University Farmers Market

WHERE: 5859 S University Blvd, Greenwood Village, CO 80121 WHEN: Every Friday 10AM to 2PM from June 5th to October 30th

South Pearl Street Farmers Market

WHERE: 1400 South Pearl Street, Denver, CO WHEN: Every Sunday 9AM to 1PM from May 17th to November 15th THINGS TO KNOW: Face masks are required.

Sloan’s Lake

Sloan’s Lake Farm & Flea

WHERE: 1565 N Raleigh St Denver, CO 80204 WHEN: Every Friday 3PM to 8PM from June 12th to September 25th THINGS TO KNOW: Masks are required and you are allowed 2 people per party. Unfortunately, dogs are no allowed at this farmers’ market. Curbside pickup is also an option, order online here.

West Colfax

West Colfax Farmers Market

WHERE: 6501-6791 W Colfax Ave, Lakewood, CO 80214 WHEN: Every Sunday 8AM to 1PM from June 14th to October 18th

Cherry Creek/Belcaro

Cherry Creek Fresh Market

WHERE: 1st Ave & University Blvd, Denver, CO 80206 WHEN: Every Saturday 8AM to 1PM from June 6th to November 14th THINGS TO KNOW: Online ordering is available for Cherry Creek Fresh Market pick up here.

The Highlands

Highlands Square Farmers Market

WHERE: W 32nd Ave & Lowell Blvd, Denver, CO 80211 WHEN: Every Sunday 9AM to 1PM from May 31st to October 11th THINGS TO KNOW: Face masks are required.
