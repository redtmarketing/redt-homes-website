Unlocking Wealth: A Rare Investment Opportunity with Bonus Depreciation + Opportunity Zone Benefits

Turnkey new construction rentals, powerful tax advantages, and long-term growth potential—all in one smart investment.

If you're seeking a tax-advantaged way to grow wealth through real estate, this is the opportunity to know about. We’re offering a rare investment structure that combines the immediate tax benefits of 100% Bonus Depreciation with the long-term wealth-building potential of the Opportunity Zone (OZ) program—all within a fully leased, new-construction townhome in a high-growth market.

The Investment at a Glance

Imagine this:

  • ✅ You invest in a brand-new townhome in a Qualified Opportunity Zone

  • ✅ You put just 25% down

  • ✅ You begin earning passive rental income on day one

  • ✅ You receive $40K+ in bonus depreciation in year one—a major tax shield

Now project that forward...

In 10 Years…

  • Your property appreciates in value

  • Rent increases enhance your cash flow

  • And on exit? 100% of your capital gains can be tax-free, thanks to OZ exclusion benefits

This isn't theory—this is a turnkey investment opportunity we’ve structured and deployed in today’s market.

What Makes This Structure So Powerful? We’ve layered together several strategies that, individually, offer great benefits—but combined, deliver exceptional returns:

  • ✔️ 100% Bonus Depreciation (fully restored in 2025)

  • ✔️ Cost Segregation Study to maximize early-year depreciation

  • ✔️ Opportunity Zone Tax Advantages

  • ✔️ New construction for low-maintenance ownership

  • ✔️ Fully leased units with 12-month tenants in place

This approach creates a true plug-and-play passive income property backed by both monthly cash flow and strategic tax planning.

Who Is This Right For?

This model is ideal for:

  • Investors with recent capital gains (from real estate, stocks, or business sales)

  • High-income earners seeking depreciation offsets

  • Buyers who want a passive, hands-off rental property

  • Anyone looking to outperform traditional real estate returns through a tax-optimized strategy

The Financial Impact

Here’s what investors can expect:

  • Year 1 cash-on-cash returns are significantly enhanced thanks to bonus depreciation

  • Long-term IRR is boosted by rent growth and appreciation

  • Exit strategy is built around a 100% tax-free gain after 10 years in the OZ

  • And yes—monthly cash flow begins immediately
