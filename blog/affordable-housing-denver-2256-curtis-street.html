Affordable Housing Opportunity: 2256 Curtis Street, Denver

Denver’s real estate market is growing fast, and affordable housing developments are more important than ever. redT Homes is proud to offer a 44-unit multifamily/condo development site in the heart of Arapahoe Square, designed for mission-driven developers and non-profits looking to make a difference.

Prime Downtown Denver Location

This property places residents at the center of Denver living:

  • 📍 Steps from Great Divide Brewing, Snooze, and Coors Field

  • 🚶‍♂️ Walkable access to LoDo, Downtown Denver, and local shops and restaurants

  • 🚇 Convenient public transit access for easy commuting across the city

Funding and Development Advantages

  • 💰 Eligible for Prop 123 funding, supporting affordable housing initiatives

  • 🏗 Ideal for non-profits or developers focused on community impact

  • 🌟 Opportunity to develop sustainable, modern multifamily housing in a highly desirable neighborhood

Make a Lasting Impact in Denver

redT Homes is looking for mission-driven organizations to bring this project to life. If your focus is on affordable housing, urban development, or community revitalization, this site provides a platform to create quality housing solutions while strengthening the neighborhood.

📞 Connect with redT Homes today to learn how your organization can take advantage of this opportunity and help shape the future of Downtown Denver housing.
