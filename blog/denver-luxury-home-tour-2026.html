Denver Luxury Home Tour 2026: A Look Back at the 7 Homes

← Back to Blog

By redT Homes Marketing · September 28, 2026 · Home Buyers Insights

The 2026 Denver Luxury Home Tour took place on Saturday, October 10, from 11 AM to 3 PM. Seven luxury homes for sale in Denver, Colorado, priced from $1.2 million to $5.5 million at the time of the tour, opened their doors for one free, self-guided afternoon. Thank you to everyone who came out, and to the builders and listing agents who hosted.

Missed it? You can still see every home on the interactive tour map, and follow redT Homes on Eventbrite to hear about the next tour.

Denver Luxury Home Tour 2026 at a Glance

  • Date: Saturday, October 10, 2026
  • Time: 11 AM to 3 PM (Mountain Time)
  • Location: Seven homes in Denver, Colorado (full list below)
  • Cost: Free
  • Format: Self-guided open houses, visit in any order
  • Tour map: redthomes.com/luxury-home-tour
  • Hosted by: redT Homes

The Luxury Homes on the Tour

This year's lineup of Denver luxury homes ranged from $1,199,000 to $5,500,000 and included our own 2442 S Saint Paul Street, a six-bedroom, eight-bathroom redT Homes new construction home with more than 8,000 square feet of living space.

  • 2442 S Saint Paul Street, Denver, CO: $5,500,000 · 6 beds, 8 baths, 8,101 sq ft
  • 2370 S Columbine Street, Denver, CO: $2,295,000 · 6 beds, 6 baths, 5,935 sq ft
  • 2339 S Saint Paul Street, Denver, CO: $3,100,000 · 5 beds, 6 baths, 5,513 sq ft
  • 2070 S Saint Paul Street, Denver, CO: $3,995,000 · 6 beds, 8 baths, 6,264 sq ft
  • 2175 S Monroe Street, Denver, CO: $3,500,000 · 4 beds, 5 baths, 7,054 sq ft
  • 3640 E Warren Avenue, Denver, CO: $2,250,000 · 6 beds, 6 baths, 5,953 sq ft
  • 2635 S Garfield Way, Denver, CO: $1,199,000 · 4 beds, 3 baths, 2,586 sq ft

Prices and details are from the MLS as of the tour and may have changed since. Check each listing for its current status.

The Interactive Tour Map

Our interactive Denver home tour map is still online. It shows every home on the tour with its price at the time of the tour, bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage, and directions.

The Sign-In Giveaway

Guests scanned a QR code to sign in at each home. Signing in at 3 or more homes entered them in the giveaway, and every home after that earned a bonus entry.
