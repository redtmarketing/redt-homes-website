Denver Luxury Home Tour 2026: 8 Luxury Homes Open October 10

← Back to Blog

By redT Homes Marketing · September 28, 2026 · Home Buyers Insights

The Denver Luxury Home Tour is a free, self-guided open house event on Saturday, October 10, 2026, from 11 AM to 3 PM. Eight luxury homes for sale in Denver, Colorado, priced from $1.2 million to $5.5 million, open their doors for one afternoon. Use the interactive tour map to plan your route, and sign in at each home for a chance to win our giveaway.

Whether you're shopping for a luxury home in Denver, planning a custom build, or simply love great architecture and interior design, this is a rare chance to walk through eight of the city's finest new construction and luxury homes in a single day, all within a short drive of each other.

Denver Luxury Home Tour 2026: Event Details

  • Date: Saturday, October 10, 2026
  • Time: 11 AM to 3 PM (Mountain Time)
  • Location: Eight homes in Denver, Colorado (full list below)
  • Cost: Free, no tickets needed
  • Format: Self-guided open houses, visit in any order
  • Tour map: redthomes.com/luxury-home-tour
  • Hosted by: redT Homes

Luxury Homes for Sale on the Tour

This year's lineup of Denver luxury homes ranges from $1,199,000 to $5,500,000 and includes our own 2442 S Saint Paul Street, a six-bedroom, eight-bathroom redT Homes new construction home with more than 8,000 square feet of living space.

  • 2442 S Saint Paul Street, Denver, CO: $5,500,000 · 6 beds, 8 baths, 8,101 sq ft
  • 2370 S Columbine Street, Denver, CO: $2,295,000 · 6 beds, 6 baths, 5,935 sq ft
  • 2339 S Saint Paul Street, Denver, CO: $3,100,000 · 5 beds, 6 baths, 5,513 sq ft
  • 2070 S Saint Paul Street, Denver, CO: $4,150,000 · 6 beds, 8 baths, 6,264 sq ft
  • 2175 S Monroe Street, Denver, CO: $3,500,000 · 4 beds, 5 baths, 7,054 sq ft
  • 3640 E Warren Avenue, Denver, CO: $2,250,000 · 6 beds, 6 baths, 5,953 sq ft
  • 2635 S Garfield Way, Denver, CO: $1,199,000 · 4 beds, 3 baths, 2,586 sq ft
  • 2451 S Adams Street, Denver, CO: $1,850,000 · 5 beds, 4 baths, 4,669 sq ft

Prices and details are from the MLS and are subject to change.

Plan Your Route with the Interactive Tour Map

Our interactive Denver home tour map shows every home with its price, bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage, and one-tap directions. The numbers on the map show a suggested route, but there's no wrong way to do the tour: start at any home and visit in any order you like. On tour day, look for our yard signs and scan the QR code to pull up the map on your phone.

Sign In at Each Home to Win the Giveaway

Every home on the tour has a sign-in sheet with a QR code. Scan it, enter your first name, last name, and email, and you're checked in in seconds.

  • Sign in at 3 or more homes to enter the giveaway.
  • Every home after that earns you a bonus entry.

Visit all eight homes and you'll have six entries in the drawing. We'll announce the winner after the tour, so follow redT Homes on Instagram and Facebook to see who wins.

Tips for Touring Luxury Open Houses in Denver

  • Open the map before you go. Save redthomes.com/luxury-home-tour on your phone so directions are one tap away.
  • Budget your time. Plan on 15 to 20 minutes per home to see all eight in four hours.
  • Bring your questions. Builders and listing agents will be on site to talk about floor plans, finishes, energy efficiency, and what it takes to build a luxury home in Denver.
  • Sign in everywhere. Every check-in counts toward your giveaway entries.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Denver Luxury Home Tour?

The Denver Luxury Home Tour is on Saturday, October 10, 2026, from 11 AM to 3 PM Mountain Time.

How much does the luxury home tour cost?

The tour is free to attend. Just show up at any of the eight homes between 11 AM and 3 PM.

How many homes are on the tour, and what are the prices?

Eight luxury homes in Denver, Colorado are on the tour, priced from $1,199,000 to $5,500,000, with 4 to 6 bedrooms and up to 8,101 square feet.

The interactive tour map at redthomes.com/luxury-home-tour shows every home with its price, specs, and directions. You can also scan the QR code on any tour yard sign.

No. The numbers on the map are only a suggested route. Start at any home and visit in any order you like.

How does the giveaway work?

Scan the sign-in QR code at each home and enter your name and email. Sign in at 3 or more homes to enter the giveaway, and every home after that earns you a bonus entry.

Who is hosting the Luxury Home Tour?

The tour is hosted by redT Homes, a Denver home builder, together with the builders and listing agents of each participating home.

See you on Saturday, October 10!
