Sloan's Lake - Where You Can Find The Best Townhomes

← Back to Blog

By redT Homes Marketing · March 9, 2021 · Home Buyers Insights

Sloan’s Lake is a community packed full of history, artistry, and exciting revitalization. New builds are going up left and right from condominiums, row homes, and most common are townhome communities that boast unique downtown and front range mountain views. Most new construction townhome communities lie in a beautiful location, typically blocks away from Denver’s 2nd largest lake, Sloan’s Lake. When you live in a townhome in the Sloan’s Lake neighborhood, you can expect fun nights out with the neighbors, morning jogs around the lake, and evening bike rides with spectacular sunsets. 

A Brief History Of Denver’s Sloan’s Lake Neighborhood

If you go back in time to 1861, we know today as Sloan’s Lake was all farmland for local grazing cattle. Farmer Thomas M. Sloan tapped into an underground aquifer while digging a well for his farm; when he woke up the next morning, the land was flooded, and all 200 acres of his farm were submerged. This is how Sloan’s Lake rose to be one of Denver’s largest lakes and one of the best happy little accidents to ever occur in Denver’s history. 

Why Live In Sloan’s Lake?

Living in Sloan’s Lake will give you the perfect balance between an urban and suburban lifestyle. Want a night out on the town? Easy— take the light rail downtown and enjoy all the downtown amenities just 10-minutes from your front door. Want an evening bike ride? Easy— take the 2.8-mile path that circles the lake, hop on the Lakewood Dry Gulch Trail a couple of blocks from Colfax, or ride over to the Platte River Trail, which can take you downtown or to nearby Morrison. 

Walkable And Safe Neighborhood

Sloan’s Lake has excellent walk and bike scores while also providing multiple forms of public transportation. Having a walk score of 69 and a bike score of 78, it is reasonable to walk out to dinner or bike to the store to grab items for dinner. The neighborhood received an overall rating of an A from Niche, which calculates safety, nightlife, and commute times. 

Within Short Distance Of Major Roads

In a neighborhood full of young professionals, distance to staple destinations in Denver is essential for those who live in the community. In Sloan’s Lake, you are just a quick 5-minute drive up Colfax to reach I-25, which can take you anywhere in the greater Denver area. This location makes it easy to get around the city but keeps you distant from the hustle and bustle of downtown Denver.

What To Do In Sloan’s Lake

There is a lot to do in Sloan’s Lake, no matter your interest. 

Participate In Outdoor Recreation

At Sloan’s Lake Park, Denver’s 2nd largest lake, you can participate in outdoor recreation activities such as biking, walking, running, and even water skiing! Aside from the park itself, numerous trails are within biking distance from your home. If you have ever wanted to pick up a new hobby, now is your shot. You will never get tired of the endless trails that will take you straight into Denver or outside the metro area to Morrison.

Enjoy Numerous Cultural Events

Sloan’s Lake Park throws multiple festivals and cultural events that celebrate the neighborhood and its diversity. One popular festival is the Colorado Dragon Boat Festival; you will see multiple decorated dragon boats that fill the lake at this festival. Denver Sloan's Lake Art & Music Festival is another famous festival that the neighborhood is proud to host; at this festival, you will get to enjoy live music and tour local art vendors’ booths. 

Visit Various Restaurants And Bars

Sloan’s Lake and neighboring suburb West Colfax are renowned for their overwhelming numbers of local breweries. New city centers are continually being built, providing residents with multiple new options to go out and grab a brew with some appetizers. If you are looking for a place to grab a nice dinner or drinks after work, you will love Sloan’s Lake’s choices. 

See A Movie At The Alamo Drafthouse Cinema 

Just a block from Sloan’s Lake Park is the Alamo Drafthouse Cinema. When you are unsure about dinner or a movie, head on over to the Alamo and roll with both! You’ll enjoy dining-in with the best movies, beer, wine, and food. 

Go See A Broncos’ Game

Only a mile away is the newly named Empower Field, which is home to the Denver Broncos. They play there in the fall and bring out a wild crowd to cheer them on! Although the 2020 season has not gone as everyone had hoped, there is always hope for a rowdy, in-person crowd next season!

Visit The Nearby Children’s Museum Of Denver

One of Denver’s best features is that there is something to do for anyone of any age— single young professionals or a young married couple with kids; you will always find a fun weekend event to attend. One of our favorite museums in Denver is the Children’s Museum of Denver, which has everything from interactive exhibits to fun outdoor displays.
