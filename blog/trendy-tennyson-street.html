Trendy Tennyson Street in Berkeley, Denver

← Back to Blog

By redT Homes Marketing · June 12, 2020 · Home Buyers Insights

There is much fun that makes up the Berkeley Neighborhood, but the signature destination of Berkeley hands down is Tennyson Street. A cultural district in Northwest Denver that is well-known for its intense promotion of small and local businesses from art galleries to micro-breweries! It is the signature destination in Berkeley— the perfect place for the first date or to meet the parents or to catch a brew after a hard day.

Tennyson Street is home to one of a kind restaurants, a handful of micro breweries, collections of boutiques, and a handful of services from barber shops to yoga studios. The community sponsors and puts on many events such as the First Friday Culture Walks, Free Outdoor Movie Nights, and the Totally Tennyson Barcrawl. Tennyson Street is the glue that holds the community together— it is the street that draws the entire neighborhood into one place and the scene that best represents the community. Whether you are a resident, visitor, or friend you will always be welcome on Tennyson Street.

Transportation to the cultural district is doable without a car, but it is much more favorable to walk and bike the area. If you are coming from out of Berkeley, getting to Tennyson Street via car is your best bet. If you live in Berkeley, there is no doubt that wherever you are located in the neighborhood you are within walking distance of Tennyson Street and all the great destinations the street offers.

From walking the street and viewing the unique murals to shopping at the art galleries, you can enjoy Tennyson Street any time of day or any time of the year!
