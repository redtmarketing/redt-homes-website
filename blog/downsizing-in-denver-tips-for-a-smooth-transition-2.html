Downsizing in Denver: Tips for a Smooth Transition

← Back to Blog

By Kevin B · May 5, 2026 · Home Buyers Insights

At some point, many homeowners decide it’s time to downsize. Often, this happens when children move out, leaving behind extra bedrooms, unused space, and more upkeep than necessary. Suddenly, a smaller home feels like the smarter and easier choice.

If you're considering downsizing in Denver, here are a few simple ways to make the process smoother.

Declutter First

Before moving, take time to let go of what you no longer need.

Old furniture, storage boxes, and sentimental items can quickly make a smaller home feel crowded. Ask yourself what truly serves a purpose in your next space.

Less clutter means more comfort.

Ask for Help

Sorting through years of belongings can be overwhelming.

Ask family members or close friends to help you decide what stays and what goes. They may also want to keep meaningful family items, helping you preserve memories without keeping everything.

Plan Ahead

Downsizing takes time.

From garage sales and donations to storage planning and preparing your current home for sale, starting early helps reduce stress and makes the move much easier.

Know What You Want

Everyone downsizes for a reason - think about what matters most in your next home.

Do you want fewer stairs? Less maintenance? No yard work? Better walkability? Defining your priorities helps you find the right fit.

Use Smart Storage Solutions

Multi-purpose furniture like storage ottomans, beds with drawers, and expandable tables can help maximize a smaller space while keeping your home functional and organized.

Expect an Adjustment Period

Moving to a smaller home is a lifestyle change.

There are many benefits - less cleaning, lower costs, and simpler living but it may take time to adjust. Give yourself grace during the transition
