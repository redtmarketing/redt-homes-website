The Best of Glendale: Home of Forest Park 12

← Back to Blog

By redT Homes Marketing · March 6, 2020 · Home Buyers Insights

What makes Glendale so great?

When you live in Glendale, you are getting the best of Cherry Creek living without the Cherry Creek price.

At redT’s premier listing in Glendale, Forest Park 12 is in a central location to everything you need. Take a short 5 minute drive up Leetsdale and you are in the upscale, lavish Cherry Creek area. In this exciting neighborhood and commercial district, you will find yourself surrounded by luxury and class. With a variety of shopping from luxurious fashion brands to independently owned boutiques to extravagant salons to a handful of delicious dining options. Enjoy all the fun that surrounds the Cherry Creek area like the yearly Cherry Creek Arts Festival, Cherry Creek North Food & Wine, Art Feast, and Winter Fest.

Take a 4-minute drive and have the Belcaro Shopping Center at your disposal. Featuring many shops and stores that provide regular services such as a King Soopers, Cherry Creek Cleaners, Target, Vitamin Cottage, PetSmart, and Walgreens. Having all the little things such as dry cleaners and a Pharmacy so close will save you much more of a hassle than you think. Errands are less of a chore when you’re central to everything.

Enough jabbing about the necessities. Let’s dive into what matters most… FOOD! Happy hour hotspots and booming breakfast & brunch spots. Here’s what we recommend:

Top 3 Happy Hour Hotspots:

Inga’s Alpine Tavern

Located just steps away from your front door at Forest Park 12 is Inga’s Alpine Tavern! A hip space that is inspired by a Swedish ski cabin makes coming to Inga’s a whole new experience- enjoy the decor, the friendly staff, and the yummy discounted happy hour drinks and apps. Enjoy life on the shaded outdoor patio and when you’re done take a very short stroll back to your own cozy lil’ home.

Jax Fish House & Oyster Bar

A short 4-minute drive away is Jax Fish House & Oyster Bar, a perfect destination if you’re looking to go out for some grub and a drink. Explore the yummy items on the happy hour menu and pair it all up with one of their special cocktails. You can’t go wrong going to Jax!

Next Door American Eatery

Roll call for all out vegans and vegetarians! If you are looking for a spot where everyone will find something that they love, then head on down to Next Door, where yummy happy hour items such as Arugula Pesto Smashers, Honey Sriracha Chicken Bites, or Roasted Veggie Nachos are available to accommodate any gluten-free, vegetarian, or vegan needs. Spice it all up with the signature happy hour cocktails such as The Pink Drink or the Orange Fashioned!

Top 3 Breakfast & Brunch Destinations:

Sam’s No. 3

As they say themselves, they are and have the cure for resting brunch face. With fun omelets such as the Denver, the Avalanche, and the Aurora you will be sure to have that resting brunch face cured!

Jax Fish House & Oyster Bar

Once again Jax hits our top 3! Enjoy selections of Crab Cake and Salmon Lox Benedicts and Shrimp and Grits to get that first craving of the day down. Indulge in Endless Bubbles for only $15.

Morning Story Restaurant 

Down the street from your home, Morning Story would be an excellent place to walk down for brunch! Perfect for a light brunch where you can be easy and get the Breakfast Combo or be wild and go for one of their deluxe waffles like the Strawberry-Banana-Walnut Waffle.

If you love the lifestyle that Glendale can provide you with then you will love our listing, Forest Park 12! Don’t miss out on an amazing opportunity to secure your dream home in a neighborhood you love. Forest Park Living

What you will experience living in Forest Park 12 is different than any place you have ever experienced before, be in the middle of it all but still have distance from the chaos and have an escape waiting for you to get home. Come discover Cherry Creek living without the Cherry Creek price at Forest Park 12.
