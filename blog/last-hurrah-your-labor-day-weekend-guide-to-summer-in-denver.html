Last Hurrah: Your Labor Day Weekend Guide to Summer in Denver

← Back to Blog

By Kevin B · August 7, 2025 · Building A Greener Colorado

Top Outdoor Things to Do in Denver This Labor Day Weekend

Labor Day weekend is your last chance to enjoy summer in Denver. From paddleboarding at Sloan’s Lake to rooftop dining, here’s what not to miss.

5 Best Hikes Near Denver for a Late-Summer Adventure

Whether you’re craving mountain air or a quick city escape, these hikes near Denver are perfect for your long weekend plans.

Featured Trails:

  • Mount Falcon Park – great views + history

  • St. Mary’s Glacier – snow and lake in one hike

  • Red Rocks Trail – close and iconic

  • Three Sisters Trail – forest + open views

  • South Valley Park – perfect for families and beginners

Denver Labor Day Events to Catch Before Summer Ends

From art festivals to jazz in the park, Denver offers plenty of free or low-cost events that align with a mindful, local lifestyle.

Paddleboard Your Way Into the Long Weekend

Make the most of Labor Day with a paddleboard session on one of Denver’s nearby lakes or reservoirs. It’s an eco-friendly, active way to unwind, connect with nature, and celebrate summer’s final stretch.

Top paddleboarding spots near Denver:

  • Sloan’s Lake – perfect for sunrise paddles right in the city

  • Chatfield Reservoir – more space and rental options

  • Bear Creek Lake Park – calm waters, ideal for beginners

  • Aurora Reservoir – clear water and scenic views

Pro tips for a green weekend on the water:

  • Bring a reusable water bottle + reef-safe sunscreen

  • Rent instead of buying to reduce gear waste

  • Pick up any trash—even if it’s not yours
