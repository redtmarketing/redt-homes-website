How to Winterize a Sustainable Home: Green Tips for November & December

Winter in Colorado can be beautiful—but it can also mean higher energy bills if your home isn’t prepared. Winterizing your home sustainably not only keeps you cozy but also reduces your environmental footprint. Here’s how to get your green home ready for November and December.

1. Upgrade Your Insulation

Proper insulation is the foundation of a sustainable winter-ready home. Look for eco-friendly options like recycled insulation or sheep’s wool insulation, which help reduce energy consumption while minimizing environmental impact. Focus on:

  • Attics and basements

  • Exterior walls

  • Crawl spaces

Learn more about eco-friendly insulation options that improve energy efficiency.

2. Seal Leaks and Drafts

Air leaks are one of the biggest culprits behind rising energy costs. A few sustainable fixes include:

  • Eco-friendly weatherstripping for doors and windows

  • Caulking gaps around windows, doors, and vents

  • Checking seals around chimneys and fireplaces

These small changes can make a big difference in your home’s comfort and energy footprint.

3. Harness Passive Solar Heating

Take advantage of the sun’s natural warmth with passive solar strategies:

  • Keep south-facing windows clean and unobstructed

  • Open curtains during sunny winter days to warm interiors naturally

  • Close curtains at night to retain heat

Passive solar design is a simple way to reduce heating energy without extra technology or costs.

4. Optimize Heating with a Smart Thermostat

A smart thermostat allows you to efficiently manage your home’s temperature, saving both energy and money. Set lower temperatures when you’re away and program gradual heating for when you return. Popular options include Nest Thermostat or Ecobee.

5. Choose Sustainable Materials

When making winter upgrades, prioritize sustainable materials:

  • Recycled or natural insulation

  • Eco-friendly weatherstripping

  • Low-VOC paints and sealants for indoor air quality

These choices keep your home warm, healthy, and environmentally responsible.

Winterizing a home doesn’t have to compromise sustainability. By upgrading insulation, sealing leaks, leveraging passive solar heat, and using smart thermostats, you can create a cozy, energy-efficient home this November and December. Small, green steps today can lead to big energy savings and a more comfortable home all winter long.
