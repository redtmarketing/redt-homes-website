🧳 The Ultimate Moving Checklist: 10 Steps to a Smooth Move

Make your next move stress-free with this step-by-step guide! From packing hacks to utility setup, here’s everything you need to know for a smooth transition into your new home. So, you’ve just purchased a beautiful new home with redT—congratulations! Whether it’s a custom-built modern home or a move-in-ready new construction, this is an exciting next chapter. And while the hardest part of the buying journey might be behind you, the transition into your new space deserves just as much planning. To help you feel confident and organized, we’ve created the ultimate moving checklist—designed to make your move as smooth as possible.

1. Create a Moving Timeline (6–8 Weeks Out)

Start by setting your move-in date and working backward. Use a digital calendar or printable checklist to map out key tasks like hiring movers, scheduling utility transfers, and notifying your current landlord (if applicable). Pro Tip: If you're moving during peak season (late spring to summer), book movers early—they fill up fast!

2. Declutter Before You Pack

Now’s the time to lighten the load. Go through each room and donate, recycle, or toss what you no longer need. You’ll save time, space, and money on moving supplies. Bonus Tip: Use donation services like Goodwill or Pickup Please for easy pickups.

3. Get Quotes from Movers or Rental Trucks

Compare at least 3 moving companies or rental truck providers. Look at reviews, insurance options, and availability. Need a place to start? Check out:
  • Two Men and a Truck
  • U-Haul
  • Bellhop Moving
Hiring Movers? Ask about services like packing, unpacking, and large-item handling.

4. Start Packing Early

Begin with items you use less frequently—seasonal clothing, décor, books, etc. Label every box with the room name and a quick inventory. Color-coded tape or stickers can speed up unloading later. Packing Hack: Keep essentials like toiletries, chargers, and a change of clothes in a "first night" box. Looking for supplies? Try The Home Depot Moving Center

5. Set Up or Transfer Utilities

About two weeks before your move, contact utility providers to transfer or set up:
  • Electricity
  • Gas
  • Water
  • Internet & Cable
  • Trash/Recycling
If you're moving to Denver, check providers like:
  • Denver Water
  • Comcast/Xfinity

6. Change Your Address

Update your address with the USPS and notify banks, credit cards, insurance providers, and any subscription services. Don’t forget your employer and voter registration! 👉 Change your address with USPS

7. Plan for Pets & Kids

Keep little ones (and fur babies) safe and calm during moving day chaos. Arrange a babysitter or pet sitter, or set up a quiet room with snacks, toys, and comfort items. Bonus Tip: Look for local pet boarding options like Camp Bow Wow or trusted sitters on Rover.

8. Take Care of Your Current Home

If you're leaving a rental, clean thoroughly to ensure your deposit return. If selling, consider a professional clean before handing over keys. Extra Tip: Take final meter readings and photos to document the home’s condition. Services like Handy can connect you with local cleaners and handymen.

9. Do a Final Walkthrough

Before you lock the door one last time, check every closet, drawer, and cabinet. Make sure nothing’s left behind—and that everything’s packed securely. Optional: Snap a few final photos for your own memories or documentation.

10. Celebrate Your New Home!

Once you're in, unpack essentials first: kitchen, bedding, and bathroom supplies. Then take a breath, pop some bubbly, and enjoy your new space. Need help settling in? Your redT Homes agent can connect you with trusted local vendors for everything from landscaping to energy-efficient upgrades.
