New Townhomes Near Tennyson Street in Denver: Thirty Eight West

← Back to Blog

By redT Homes Marketing · October 22, 2026 · Home Buyers Insights

Looking for new townhomes near Tennyson Street in Denver? Thirty Eight West, a new construction townhome community by redT Homes at West 38th Avenue and Perry Street, sits about four blocks east of Tennyson Street in the Berkeley neighborhood. Homes range from 1,168 to 3,011 square feet with two to five bedrooms, attached garages, and no HOA dues, and the community has a Walk Score of 93.

Below is what’s available now, what makes living near Tennyson different, and what to look for when you compare new townhomes in northwest Denver.

Key takeaways

  • Location: Thirty Eight West is at W 38th Ave and Perry St, about four blocks from Tennyson Street, with Highlands Square, Berkeley Park, and Rocky Mountain Lake Park nearby.
  • Homes: 1,168 to 3,011 sq ft, 2 to 5 bedrooms, every home with an attached garage. Some have rooftop decks with mountain views.
  • Pricing (as of October 2026): from $540,000 to $1,300,000.
  • Ownership: no HOA dues. Shared items are covered by a party wall agreement.
  • Status: under construction, with 2026 completion.

Why buyers want to live near Tennyson Street

Tennyson Street is the main street of Denver’s Berkeley neighborhood. The stretch from about 38th to 46th Avenue is packed with independent restaurants, coffee shops, breweries, galleries, boutiques, and fitness studios, and it hosts neighborhood events through the year. At the north end, Berkeley Park and its lake add trails, a dog park, and open space.

The appeal is simple: you can walk to dinner, coffee, and the park, and still be about 15 minutes from downtown. Older Berkeley homes are mostly early 1900s bungalows and Tudors, so new construction near Tennyson is limited. That’s a big part of why new townhomes here get attention.

A home for every stage

The two-bedroom plans work well for first-time buyers and downsizers who want to own near Tennyson without taking on a full house. The larger homes fit families who need room to grow. The five-bedroom home at #107 has a true lock-off suite with its own bedroom, bath, and kitchen, which makes it a strong fit for multigenerational living, long-term guests, or rental income.

No HOA dues

Thirty Eight West doesn’t have a homeowners association. Instead, a party wall agreement covers the shared walls between homes. That means no monthly HOA dues, though it’s worth reading the agreement so you know what you’re responsible for.

What’s within walking distance

  • Tennyson Street – about four blocks west for restaurants, coffee, shops, and breweries
  • Highlands Square – more dining and shopping around 32nd Avenue and Lowell Boulevard
  • Rocky Mountain Lake Park – a lake loop, playground, and open space to the north
  • Berkeley Park – Berkeley Lake, trails, and a dog park at the north end of Tennyson
  • Downtown Denver – about 15 minutes by car, with quick access to I-70 and I-25

What to look for in new townhomes near Tennyson Street

  1. Garage parking. Street parking near Tennyson fills up on weekends. An attached garage, especially a two-car garage, is worth a lot here.
  2. Usable outdoor space. Rooftop decks in Berkeley can catch mountain views. Ask about size, access, and privacy.
  3. HOA or party wall agreement. Know your monthly costs and what’s covered before you compare prices.
  4. Light and layout. Townhomes share walls, so end units and window placement make a big difference.
  5. Who built it. Ask who designed the home and how it was built. Our 11 questions to ask a Denver townhome builder is a good checklist.
