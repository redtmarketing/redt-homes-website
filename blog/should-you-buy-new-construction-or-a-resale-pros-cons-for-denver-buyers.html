Should You Buy New Construction or a Resale? Pros & Cons for Denver Buyers

If you're house hunting in the Denver market, you've probably noticed two very different types of homes hitting your feed: brand-new builds and resale homes with character. Both have their advantages—and the right choice depends on your lifestyle, priorities, and budget. So, which one’s right for you? Let’s break down the pros and cons.

🔨 New Construction Homes

These are newly built properties, often with modern finishes, energy efficiency, and minimal maintenance. At redT Homes, we specialize in building sustainable, design-forward homes throughout the Denver metro area—like our current listing at:

🏡 2442 S Saint Paul St | Denver

8,100+ sq ft | Elevator | Finished Basement | 3-Car Garage | Completion: August 2025 Still time to pick select finishes! Dublin Development

✅ Pros:

  • Customization: Pick your finishes, layouts, and even floorplan tweaks (if you buy early enough).
  • Energy Efficiency: Modern systems = lower utility bills. Many redT homes are LEED or ENERGY STAR Certified.
  • Low Maintenance: Everything is brand-new and under warranty.
  • Modern Design: Open layouts, smart home tech, and up-to-date materials.

❌ Cons:

  • Less Charm: You won’t find original woodwork or mature landscaping.
  • Construction Zones: In newer developments, your dream home may come with temporary noise and dust next door.

🏘️ Resale Homes

These are existing homes—some turnkey, some with renovation potential—ranging from mid-century ranches to charming bungalows and Victorians.

Check out this beautifully updated gem in West Highland:

🏡 3922 W 29th Ave | Denver

3 Beds | 3 Baths | Timeless charm in the heart of West Highland
Close to Sloan's Lake, shops, and restaurants. A classic Denver home with thoughtful updates.

✅ Pros:

  • Established Neighborhoods: Mature trees, walkable blocks, and community vibes.

  • Bigger Yards/Lots: Older homes often sit on more generous parcels.

  • Character & History: Architectural details you just can’t replicate.

  • Negotiation Power: More flexibility on price, concessions, and close dates.

❌ Cons:

  • Older Systems: May need roof, HVAC, or electrical updates.

  • Style Mismatch: You might spend extra to modernize kitchens, baths, etc.

  • Less Energy Efficient: Insulation, windows, and appliances may need upgrading.

🔍 Which One’s Right for You?

Here’s a quick decision checklist:
Question If YES, consider...
Want a low-maintenance home for years to come? ✅ New Build
Love homes with charm and character? ✅ Resale
Want to pick your own finishes or floorplan? ✅ New Build
Looking for walkability in historic neighborhoods? ✅ Resale
Prioritizing energy efficiency or sustainability? ✅ New Build
Want to get more square footage for your budget? ✅ Resale
