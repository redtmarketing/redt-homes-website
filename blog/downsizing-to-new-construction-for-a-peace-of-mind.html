Downsizing to New Construction for a Peace of Mind

← Back to Blog

By redT Homes Marketing · July 12, 2019 · Home Buyers Insights

The time has come to downsize!

Maybe your children are going off to school, and you no longer need the giant yard and responsibilities that come with it, or maybe you just want to get out of the ‘burbs’ and into the city.

Whatever the case may be, downsizing into a New Construction home can offer so many more benefits than downsizing to an older home. As we mentioned in our last post: 8 Reasons to Buy New Construction, new construction homes are easier to maintain then older homes, because everything is new!

New construction homes offer new features that aren’t seen in older homes before upgrading: new smart features, larger and more functional kitchen spaces, and open floorplans. Even if you’re downsizing to a home with less rooms, the open floorplans make it feel like you aren’t losing a ton of space.

Downsizing eliminates those chores such as mowing the lawn, pulling the weeds, and fixing the sprinkler system. We call it the “Lock Up and Leave Lifestyle”, perfect for world travelers and city-lovers.

redT sells new construction townhomes and condos that are all excellent options for anyone looking to downsize. If you are looking to have a better home experience and less maintenance, downsizing to new construction could be the right choice for you.
