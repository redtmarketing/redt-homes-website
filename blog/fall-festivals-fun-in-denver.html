Fall Festivals & Fun in Denver

← Back to Blog

By Kevin B · September 17, 2025 · Home Buyers Insights

🍂 South Pearl Farmers’ Market

Every Sunday through mid-November, the South Pearl Farmers’ Market brings together local farmers, artisans, and food vendors. Stroll through the charming neighborhood streets while picking up fresh produce, handmade goods, and seasonal treats like cider and pumpkin bread. It’s the perfect way to support local businesses while enjoying the autumn vibes.

🧺 Pumpkin Patch: Maize in the City (Thornton) & More

No fall roundup is complete without a pumpkin patch! Here are a few great ones around Denver:

 

    • Maize in the City (Thornton) — Corn maze, mini maze, petting zoo, and a wide variety of pumpkins. (maizeinthecity.com)

    • Rock Creek Farm (Broomfield) — Over 80 acres of pumpkin fields, hay bale mazes, and corn mazes. (rockcreekfarm.com)

    • Mile High Farms (Bennett) — Pumpkin patch, corn maze, hayrides, and a petting zoo. (milehighfarms.com)

    • Anderson Farms (Erie) — U-pick pumpkin patch, giant corn maze, wagon rides, and family activities. (andersonfarms.com)

👻 Elitch Gardens Fright Fest

Ready for a scare? Head to Elitch Gardens Fright Fest, where the amusement park transforms into a haunted wonderland. By day, kids can enjoy trick-or-treating and family rides, while at night, haunted houses and scare zones take over for thrill-seekers.

🍴 Best Places to Eat

After a day of fall adventures, cozy up at one of Denver’s seasonal dining spots:

  • Snooze — Popular brunch spot with fall-inspired pancakes and seasonal cocktails. (snoozeeatery.com)

  • Mercantile Dining & Provision — Seasonal American comfort food in Union Station. (mercantiledenver.com)

  • Linger — Rooftop dining in LoHi, perfect for sunset and fall evenings. (lingerdenver.com)

  • The Pig & The Sprout — Cozy seasonal dishes with vegetarian and meat options. (pigandsprout.com)

  • Punch Bowl Social — Casual dining with a fireplace and games for all ages. (punchbowlsocial.com)

🌟 Weekend Adventures & Local Fun

  • Explore city parks with fall foliage: Washington Park, Cheesman Park, and City Park are gorgeous this time of year.

  • Take a brewery tour: Try seasonal pumpkin ales at Great Divide Brewing, Denver Beer Co, or Wynkoop Brewing.

  • Attend fall festivals: Check local event calendars for craft fairs, harvest festivals, and family-friendly Halloween events.

Denver in the fall is all about community, creativity, and seasonal celebrations. From exploring local art to picking pumpkins, enjoying delicious seasonal food, or braving a haunted corn maze, there’s no shortage of ways to make the most of autumn in the Mile High City.
