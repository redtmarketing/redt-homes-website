Best of Tennyson Street: Berkeley, Denver

← Back to Blog

By redT Homes Marketing · March 27, 2020 · Uncategorized

What are some of the best things about Berkeley?

Simple, Tennyson Street!

This northwest neighborhood is located just 15-minutes outside of downtown Denver and promotes a vibrant way of life for anyone that lives or visits. Tennyson Street in Berkeley is notorious for its local lifestyle and mom n’ pop shops. Walkability and convenience is best in Berkeley and even better on Tennyson Street.

In this neighborhood and business district, you will find yourself surrounded by culture and community. With a variety of people, stores, and restaurants, you are sure to find your forever home in Berkeley. In our newest listing in Berkeley, the 44th + Tennyson Townhomes, you will find yourself right on Tennyson Street! In this amazing location you can be walking distance from anything you may ever need. Walk 10-minutes up 44th Street and you will find a Safeway, walk 6-minutes up Tennyson and you will find Berkeley Lake Park, and walk 30-seconds out your door and you will be right at Parisi Pizzeria. Location is everything and Tennyson Street is everything! Here are some of our favorite restaurants and shops on Tennyson Street:

Top 3 Brunch Destinations:

Denver Biscuit Co.

Take your breakfast biscuits to a whole new level at the Denver Biscuit Co.! From massive biscuits such as The Dahlia, The Franklin, and The Cordon Bleu to biscuit plates such as the Biscuits & Gravy and the Biscuit Pot Pie. There is an option for everyone at the Denver Biscuit Co., don’t be a stranger and head on over!

Cozy Cottage

At this chill, homey eatery, you will find a mix of American and Mexican food for both breakfast and lunch. Featuring a seasonal patio, perfect for brunch in the spring and fall! This local farm to table restaurant has sustainable practices and fresh local food whatever the season.

Revelry Kitchen

This family owned and operated business is right on Tennyson Street and features a scratch menu made with fresh ingredients. This dream brunch restaurant features a wide menu ranging from benedicts to pancakes and french toast to sandwiches! They also have an amazing drink menu with drinks such as the Hibiscus Paloma and the Blue Gray Mimosa. Come find your new favorite Berkeley Brunch!

Top 3 Happy Hour Hot Spots:

Local 46

At this local bar and biergarten find local events being put on nearly 6 time a week! This bar is isolated on the North end of Tennyson Street, right across the street from Berkeley Lake Park and has corn hole, a bocce court, ping pong tables, and fire pits that are open year-round! Have fun anytime of year at Local 46. Happy hour is from 3-6pm Monday through Friday!

Hops & Pie

This local brew and pizzeria is right on Tennyson Street and is always the talk of the town. Their monthly pies have the most unique combination of toppings and will leave you coming back again. Stop on by for the Tuesday “Twenty-Three” where you can get an 18' pie and two drafts for $23! Happy hour is Tuesday through Friday from 3-6:30pm!

Berkeley Untapped

This is the go-to bar for local beers on tap! This bar is the best place to get local beer, wine, and whiskey— variety is their game, expect new brews every time you walk in! With an outdoor patio, find a taste in the life of living on Tennyson Street, there is no better place that represents the best community feel. Happy hour is Monday through Friday from 4-6pm!
