Denver Tech Center - History & Modern Living At Villa Rosso

← Back to Blog

By redT Homes Marketing · February 9, 2021 · Home Buyers Insights

When you live at The Flats at VillaRosso, you are truly living there for the neighborhood. The Denver Tech Center is well known for having high-end condominium communities that are for-sale or for-lease. You get the best of both worlds at The Flats at VillaRosso; because of its location, you are out of the way from any busy roads and traffic, but a 5-minute walk to the Shops & Restaurants at Belleview Station and the Belleview Station Lightrail stop. The Flats at VillaRosso give you everything you want in a suburban neighborhood with an urban neighborhood’s amenities.

A Brief History Of The DTC

The Denver Tech Center started with the same purpose it provides today— to be a destination for companies to headquarter or have offices. It was established in 1962 and designed by architect Carl A. Worthington to create a center point where people could live and work effectively. Since its development in the early 60s, massive companies such as Oracle Corp., Comcast, and Newmont Mining Corporation have established offices. 

Why Live In The DTC?

It is the question you should always ask before you make a move. Why should I live here? The answer is simple: the DTC has nearly 35,000 Denver citizens who commute to work each day. If you work in the DTC, why not live 5-minutes away from work? It is an excellent neighborhood that allows for a quick commute to work that avoids all the busy roads, and is the perfect place to live to catch happy hour on your way home from work. Restaurants in the Denver Tech Center can range from Ruth’s Chris, one of the best steakhouses in the US, or quick lunches at nice salad bars. Not to mention, the entertainment that you can find in the DTC is unmatched— Comedy Works or catching a movie will become one of your favorite weekend outings. 

What To Do Near The DTC

The Denver Tech Center is full of restaurants, entertainment, and parks that make it one of the hottest neighborhoods in south Denver. 

Spend An Afternoon At Cherry Creek Park

Cherry Creek State Park is just 10-minutes outside of the DenverTech Center and offers endless activities year-round. You can engage in-park activities such as bike riding around the 12-miles of paved trails or the 35-miles of multi-use trails. You could take on bird watching as a new hobby, or even cross-country ski once it starts snowing. Other water activities in the park include boating, waterskiing, jet-skiing, and fishing! 

Enjoy Numerous Restaurants And Bars

There are three major centers at the Denver Tech Center: The Shops & Restaurants at Belleview Station, The Landmark, and Marina Square and Shopping Center. You will find a variety of dining options that work perfectly for any occasion. Sushi-Rama, Zeal, Lucy, Shannahan’s Steakhouse, and Ruth’s Chris are some of the best restaurants in the area. 

Check Out Some Music At The Fiddler’s Green Amphitheatre

Way back when in early 2020, there were concerts held at this outdoor theater in Englewood, CO. It has an 18,000-person capacity and is well known for having folk bands, country artists, and pop artists who stop through for a fun night in Denver. Get ready for 2021 because many concerts have been rescheduled, and the venue will need local’s support to continue to thrive. 

Play Golf Or Tennis

The DTC is a higher-end neighborhood that is surrounded by multiple country clubs and golf courses. Within a 10-mile radius of the DTC, there are eight well-maintained golf courses and perfect for any level. There is also Club Greenwood and the Colorado Athletic Club with tennis courts if golf isn’t your thing. 

See A Movie At The Landmark At Greenwood Village

The Landmark is a movie theater in Greenwood Village that features independent movies and foreign cinema for a great price! They have outstanding service and even give customers complimentary popcorn and drinks. If you want to ditch your traditional movie experience, head on down to The Landmark to have a relaxing evening. 

About The Flats At Villa Rosso 

The Flats at VillaRosso is an impressive collection of new construction, for-sale condos in a quiet corner of the Denver Tech Center. The Flats neighbors VillaRosso, so you will have a fantastic community of neighbors that get together and throw events. Inside, you will find beautiful, modern kitchens and living rooms with a ton of space! The best part of the Flats is the master bedroom; there are walk-in showers with custom tile shelving, dual sinks, and easy access to the massive closet, we mean massive! It is a beautiful community full of even better people; we invite you to see for yourself. 

Live At The Flats At Villa Rosso And Enjoy All That The DTC Has To Offer

Discover life in the Denver Tech Center and discover the joys of living at The Flats at VillaRosso. Spend a day at Cherry Creek State Park, take your visitors to a show at Comedy Works, or have a fun date night at Sushi-Rama! The possibilities are endless when you settle down in a great neighborhood in an even better condo!
