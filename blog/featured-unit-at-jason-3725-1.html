Featured Unit at JASON: 3725 #1

← Back to Blog

By redT Homes Marketing · August 14, 2020 · Home Buyers Insights

As you have heard, redT’s latest project is Jason. This brand new townhome community is located in the Lower Highlands, better known as LoHi. This featured unit at Jason is an end unit, in the rear building that provides seclusion and privacy as it is nestled in a quiet corner of LoHi. In LoHi, you will find a perfect mix of new construction and classic bungalow-style homes that give the neighborhood a unique style that you will quickly notice as you walk through the neighborhood. The Lower Highlands is one of the most sought after neighborhoods in Denver and ranks #8 out of all Denver neighborhoods according to 5280 Magazine. When you live at Jason, you will learn to love the little things in life— Bernabe "Indio" Franco Park around the corner, the city views, and the weekends on the roof deck at Avanti!

Let’s dive in and take a look at our breathtaking property 3725 #1 at Jason…

3725 #1 Jason Street Denver, CO 80211

Front view of 3725 Jason Street, Unit #1 is the first white unit

Beautiful modern kitchens with two-tone cabinetry and matte black plumbing fixtures

 

3725 #1 at Jason is an amazing opportunity, it is not only an end unit but it is also located in the back of the project so it provides much more privacy and light compared to the other units in the community. Some unique aspects of this unit at Jason are:

  • Utility Closet on First Level: There is no need for the water heater to be on the living or bedroom level, we can save you the awkward conversation of, “Is this the bathroom? Oh wait, it’s the water heater”. Keep the utilities in the garage!
  • His & Hers Walk-In Closets: There is no reason to fight over who gets what rack, get your own master bedroom walk-in closet!
  • Roofdeck with Views: One of the best characteristics of townhomes built in LoHi is that they boast incredible downtown Denver views!
  • Unique Finish Packages: In a time where many townhomes have the same design patterns, this unit at Jason has a new finish package that has not been used in redT townhome projects before! Our architecture team designed the two-tone cabinets to be navy and a light grain wood while also including matte black plumbing and lighting fixtures throughout the home.

Are you interested in seeing and learning more about this unit at Jason? Our sales team is available any day of the week to give you an exclusive tour! Discover home in the Lower Highlands at Jason.
