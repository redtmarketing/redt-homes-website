Reasons to Buy a Home in Denver's Berkeley Neighborhood

There are many amazing neighborhoods in and around Denver, but one that stands out is Denver’s Berkeley Neighborhood. Its interesting architecture, walkability, and dog friendliness make it a highly desirable neighborhood for people interested in buying a new home in Denver. Read below to see our top three reasons to buy a home in Denver’s Berkeley neighborhood.

Tennyson Street

Tennyson Street is full of exciting restaurants and shops that will appeal to anyone in the area. Whether you visit Local 46, Berkeley Untapped, or eclectic gift shops, there is something for everyone. Berkeley is also extremely walkable and safe overall. It is a great neighborhood for young families and professionals looking to be close to the action while still having tranquility.

Interesting Architecture

Berkeley is known for its beautiful and unique architecture. You will find old Tudor homes mixed in with new, modern properties. This interesting combination of old and new makes for a well-rounded neighborhood. Overall, it is perfect for those looking to buy a home in the Denver area.

Dog Friendly

The Berkeley neighborhood has one of the best dog parks in Denver. Beyond the designated dog park, though, there are numerous green spaces available for you and your furry friend to enjoy. The neighborhood itself is extremely dog friendly, which means you can enjoy all the amenities of Berkeley with your dog at your side.
