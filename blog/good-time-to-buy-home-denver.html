← Back to Blog

By Kevin B · January 30, 2026 · Home Buyers Insights

Is Now a Good Time to Buy a Home in Denver? A Smart Buyer’s 2026 Guide

If you’ve been watching the Denver market over the past few years, you probably remember how intense it felt. Bidding wars. Waived inspections. Offers far over asking just to compete.

That’s not today’s market.

And that shift is exactly why now is a good time to buy a home in Denver.

At redT Homes, we’re seeing buyers succeed in ways that simply weren’t possible a few years ago - because conditions now favor prepared, strategic buyers.

Buyers Finally Have Options Again

Inventory has increased from record lows. That means:

  • More homes to choose from

  • Less pressure to make split-second decisions

  • Time to compare properties and neighborhoods

  • The ability to be selective instead of reactive

You can actually shop for a home again - instead of racing for one.

Negotiating Power Is Back in Buyers’ Hands

This is the biggest change in the Denver real estate market.

Today, buyers are successfully negotiating:

  • Purchase price

  • Inspection items and repairs

  • Seller-paid closing costs

  • Interest rate buydowns

Those opportunities were nearly impossible during the frenzy years. Now, they’re common - especially on homes that have been on the market for a few weeks.

Less Competition = Better Decisions

Fewer bidding wars means you can:

  • Keep inspection contingencies

  • Avoid overpaying out of urgency

  • Make decisions based on value, not pressure

  • Protect your long-term investment

This creates a healthier environment to buy a home in Denver.

Prices Are Stabilizing - Not Skyrocketing

Many buyers are waiting for prices to drop. What’s actually happening is more useful:

  • Overpriced homes sit

  • Well-priced homes sell at fair value

  • Buyers can identify real opportunities without panic

You’re purchasing based on value, not market hype.

Neighborhoods Where Buyers Are Finding Value

Buyers are finding strong opportunities in:

  • Washington Park

  • Berkeley

  • Sloan's Lake

  • Platt Park

  • Highlands

These neighborhoods continue to offer the lifestyle, walkability, and long-term value buyers want - without the chaos of past years.

What Smart Buyers Are Doing Right Now

  • Targeting homes on the market 2+ weeks

  • Asking for concessions

  • Comparing multiple options before offering

  • Prioritizing long-term value over short-term rate headlines

  • Working with local experts who understand where leverage exists

Is Now a Good Time to Buy a Home in Denver?

Yes - because this market rewards preparation and strategy.

You have options.
You have leverage.
You have time to make a smart decision.

And with the right plan, you can buy a home on terms that simply weren’t possible a few years ago.

If you’re wondering how to take advantage of today’s Denver housing market, the team at redT Homes is here to help you build a plan that works for you.
