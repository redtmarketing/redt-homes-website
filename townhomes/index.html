Questions Buyers Ask

Frequently asked questions

How much do new townhomes cost in Denver?

redT's townhomes for sale right now range from $540,000 to $1,300,000, depending on size, location and finishes. Each listing has current pricing and a payment estimate.

Are redT townhomes new construction?

Yes. Every redT townhome is new construction, available move-in ready or as a pre-construction opportunity while it's being built.

Where are redT's townhomes?

Current redT townhomes are in Thirty Eight West, Lafayette Townhomes, Sherman Townhomes and 44th Avenue Townhomes, in established Denver neighborhoods.

Do townhomes have an HOA?

Many townhome communities do, and what the HOA covers varies from building to building. Ask for the monthly dues, the budget and a list of what's covered before you buy, and we're happy to walk you through it for any redT home.
