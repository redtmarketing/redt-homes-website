Why buyers choose a paired home

Single-family feel

Your own entrance and your own space, with just one shared wall and one neighbor on that side.

A more attainable price

Sharing a wall lets you get a newer, larger home in an established neighborhood for less than a comparable detached house.

Less to maintain

New construction means current systems and finishes, and a paired home has less exterior to care for than a detached home.

Built by a team with a LEED record

The same team designs, builds and sells every home, with standards shaped by years of LEED-certified building. More about redT.

Questions Buyers Ask

Frequently asked questions

What is a paired home?

A paired home is two homes built side by side that share one common wall. It's often called a duplex. Each home has its own entrance and living space.

What's the difference between a paired home and a townhome?

A townhome is usually one of several homes in a row, with neighbors on one or both sides. A paired home shares just one wall with one neighbor. See redT townhomes.

How much do paired homes cost in Denver?

redT's paired homes for sale right now range from $550,000 to $685,000, depending on size and finishes.
