About redT Homes

Colorado living, built with intent.

redT Homes designs and builds new townhomes, paired homes (duplexes) and single-family homes across Denver and the Front Range, for homebuyers looking for a thoughtfully built place to call home, and development partners looking for a disciplined build team.

We’re a Denver-based team that handles land, design, construction, and sales in-house, led by the people who scope every project and see it through to the final walkthrough. And we build with a track record to back it up: LEED Gold and Platinum certified homes, and LEED Zero certified homes, verified by third parties, not just claimed in a brochure.

What We Build

Townhomes

New townhomes in established Denver neighborhoods, with modern, livable floor plans, natural light, and outdoor space like rooftop decks where the site allows. See townhomes for sale.

Paired homes

Paired homes (duplexes) that give you the feel of a single-family home, with your own entry and private space, at a more attainable price point. See paired homes for sale.

Single-family homes

Single-family homes in Denver and along the Front Range, each designed for its lot and the neighborhood around it.

Move-in ready & pre-construction

Buy a finished home that’s ready now, or get in early on a home still being built, matched to the neighborhood and floor plan that fit how you live.

Our Building Record

Thoughtfully built, and proven.

For years, redT built its LiteHomes to LEED Gold certification standards or higher, verified by the U.S. Green Building Council, with EPA Indoor airPLUS certification for healthier indoor air. In 2022 we delivered LEED Zero certified homes, homes verified to produce as much energy as they use.

Homes like our LEED-certified LiteHome on S Dahlia Street show what that looks like in practice: modern design, natural light, and a home built well enough to prove it to a third party.

That experience shapes every home we build today, certified or not: careful construction, efficient systems, and materials chosen to last. It’s what we mean by a thoughtfully built home.

Questions We Hear

Frequently asked questions

What kind of homes does redT build?

New townhomes, paired homes (duplexes), and single-family homes across Denver and the Front Range, available move-in ready or as pre-construction opportunities.

Where does redT build?

Mainly in established Denver neighborhoods, with homes along Colorado’s Front Range as well. Current homes for sale are listed on our homepage.

Is redT a green builder?

We have a strong green-building record, including LEED Gold and Platinum certified homes and LEED Zero certified homes. Today that experience shows up as build quality in every home: tight construction, efficient systems, and attention to indoor air.

How is redT different from other Denver builders?

We handle land, design, construction, and sales in-house, and the people who scope each project see it through to the walkthrough. Our construction standards come from years of building to third-party LEED certification.

Does redT work with landowners and developers?

Yes. We offer land acquisition, development, and architecture partnership services for landowners and development groups building across Colorado.
