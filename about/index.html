About redT Homes

Design-forward townhomes, built to a higher standard.

redT Homes is a Denver homebuilder that designs and builds modern townhomes in the city’s established urban neighborhoods. We build on infill lots, a few homes at a time, and design each one for its site: open, light-filled floor plans, clean modern lines, quality finishes, and outdoor space like rooftop decks that make city living feel bigger.

Behind the design is a team with a serious building record. Before most Denver builders were talking about it, redT was building LEED Gold and Platinum certified homes, and we have delivered LEED Zero certified homes. That experience is why our homes are built the way they are, even when certification isn’t the headline.

What Sets a redT Home Apart

Designed for the site

Every redT home starts with its lot. Our in-house design team plans each floor plan and exterior around light, views, and how the home will actually be lived in, instead of repeating the same plan down the street.

Modern, livable layouts

Open main levels, big windows, thoughtful storage, and rooftop decks and private outdoor space where the site allows. Modern design that works for everyday life, not just for photos.

In the neighborhoods people want

We build infill homes in established Denver neighborhoods close to parks, restaurants, transit, and downtown, instead of large subdivisions at the edge of the metro.

Built by a team with a LEED track record

Years of building to third-party LEED standards shaped how we build every home: tight construction, efficient systems, and attention to indoor air quality. You get the benefit of that experience whether or not a home is certified.

One team, start to finish

Land, design, construction, and sales are all handled in-house, so the people who designed your home are the same people who answer your questions about it.

Our Building Record

Where our standards come from.

For years, redT built its LiteHomes to LEED Gold certification standards or higher, verified by the U.S. Green Building Council, with EPA Indoor airPLUS certification for healthier indoor air. In 2022 we delivered LEED Zero certified homes, homes verified to produce as much energy as they use.

Homes like our LEED-certified LiteHome on S Dahlia Street show what that looks like in practice: modern design, natural light, and a home built well enough to prove it to a third party.

Today our focus is design. We bring the same building discipline to every home: careful construction, efficient systems, and materials chosen to last. It just isn’t the only thing we talk about anymore.

Questions Buyers Ask

Frequently asked questions

What kind of homes does redT build?

Modern townhomes, paired homes, and single-family homes in established Denver neighborhoods. Our focus today is design-forward townhomes on urban infill lots: open, light-filled floor plans, quality finishes, and features like rooftop decks, close to the places people want to live.

Is redT a green builder?

We have a deep green-building record, including LEED Gold and Platinum certified homes and LEED Zero certified homes. Today we lead with design, and that experience shows up as build quality: tight construction, efficient systems, and attention to indoor air.

How is redT different from other Denver townhome builders?

We handle land, design, construction, and sales in-house and build a few homes at a time on infill lots, not large subdivisions. Each home is designed for its site, and our construction standards come from years of building to third-party LEED certification.

What should I ask a builder before buying?

Ask who designed the home and whether the plan was made for that site, what the warranty covers, what the HOA covers, how the home is insulated and heated, whether any part of the build was third-party verified, and to see finished homes. We’re happy to walk you through every one of these.
