About This Home

Tastefully remodeled and thoughtfully updated, this mid-century modern home offers approximately 4,041 finished square feet with five bedrooms, four bathrooms, a dedicated office, and flexible living spaces across three levels. The main level features engineered hardwood flooring, two bedrooms, abundant storage, and a chef's kitchen with quartz countertops and full-height backsplash, soft-close solid-wood cabinetry, two pantries, a beverage refrigerator, and high-end Bosch appliances including a 36-inch induction range with downdraft ventilation, double ovens, microwave, dishwasher, and large refrigerator. The expansive upper-level primary retreat features vaulted ceilings, space for a sitting or fitness area, dual custom walk-in closets, and a spa-inspired bath with designer finishes, freestanding soaking tub, steam shower with dual shower heads, bidet wiring, and space for stacked laundry. An adjoining skylit loft offers additional flexible space for a reading nook, workout area, or storage. The finished basement includes two additional bedrooms, a spacious family room ideal for games or a home theater, a kitchenette, and a full laundry room with prep and storage space. Additional highlights include a new gas fireplace, designer tile and lighting, updated windows, LED recessed lighting with night-light settings, luxury vinyl plank, and designer Berber carpet. Outside, enjoy a Trex deck, new cedar fencing, sprinkler system, native Colorado landscaping, oversized detached two-car garage with alley access, front driveway storage for a boat or recreational vehicle, and a large storage shed. Major improvements include a new roof, updated electrical panel, high-efficiency HVAC system, and heat-pump water heater. Ideally located near Sloan's Lake with convenient access to parks, dining, shopping, and downtown Denver.
