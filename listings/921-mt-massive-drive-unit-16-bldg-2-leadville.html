About This Townhome

This beautifully remodeled 2-story townhome in the mountain town of Leadville features an eat-in kitchen with quartz countertops, new appliances, and bar-top seating, plus two covered decks with mountain views and a fenced rear patio. Includes 3 off-street parking spaces, an under-stair storage closet, and a walk-in closet. Walking distance to downtown Leadville's shops and restaurants and adjacent to the Mineral Belt recreational trail, with Ski Cooper just 10 minutes away. HOA covers electric, gas, heat, water, sewer, snow removal, trash, grounds maintenance, insurance, and management.
