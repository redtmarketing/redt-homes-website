Why redT

Reimagining Homebuilding for a Healthier Future

Every redT home is built to a higher standard, verified by independent certifications — not just claimed in marketing copy. Here's exactly what that means, and why it matters to you.

All-Electric Living

No gas lines. No combustion. Just cleaner air and lower bills.

Every redT home runs entirely on electricity — heating, cooling, water heating, and cooking. There's no gas line to install, maintain, or worry about leaking.

Why it matters to you: combustion appliances release pollutants indoors and are a leading source of home safety incidents. Going all-electric removes that risk entirely, while efficient heat pump systems typically cost less to run than gas over time.

Healthier Indoor Air

Built around how your family actually breathes

Window placement, humidity-balanced HVAC, and low-VOC material selection aren't afterthoughts — they're designed in from the first floor plan, then verified by EPA Indoor airPlus certification.

Why it matters to you: indoor air is often more polluted than outdoor air. For families managing allergies, asthma, or just wanting a cleaner home, this is a health decision as much as a building one.

Energy Efficiency

ENERGY STAR certified, top to bottom

From insulation and window performance to the mechanical systems themselves, every redT home is verified by an independent ENERGY STAR rater — not just built to code minimums.

Why it matters to you: lower energy use means a lower monthly bill for as long as you own the home, and a stronger resale story when you eventually sell.

Water Efficiency

WaterSense fixtures throughout

Every faucet, showerhead, and toilet in a redT home is WaterSense certified, using at least 20% less water than standard fixtures without sacrificing performance.

Why it matters to you: in a state where water matters, using less of it is both a household savings and an environmental one.

Attainable Pricing

Priced at or below the neighborhood median

Right-sized, thoughtfully designed homes let redT deliver new construction at or below the median price for the neighborhood — instead of building the largest, most expensive home a lot allows.

Why it matters to you: you get new construction, healthier air, and lower operating costs without a new-construction price premium.

The Proof

Every claim, independently certified

ENERGY STAR Certified

A federal program verifying a home's insulation, windows, and mechanical systems meet strict energy-efficiency requirements, independently inspected and tested.

EPA Indoor airPlus

Builds on ENERGY STAR with additional requirements for moisture control, ventilation, and low-emission materials to protect indoor air quality.

WaterSense Certified Fixtures

An EPA program certifying fixtures that use at least 20% less water than standard models while maintaining performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are all-electric homes reliable through a Colorado winter?

Yes. redT homes use modern electric heat pump systems designed for Denver's climate, paired with tight, well-insulated building envelopes. The result is more consistent indoor temperatures than many older gas-heated homes, without a gas line to maintain.

Do all-electric homes cost more than standard new construction?

redT prices homes at or slightly below the neighborhood median. The certifications add long-term value through lower utility bills and stronger resale demand, not a higher purchase price.

How much can I expect to save on utility bills?

Savings vary by home and usage, but ENERGY STAR certified, all-electric homes with WaterSense fixtures typically use significantly less energy and water than comparable resale homes, which lowers monthly utility costs.

What is EPA Indoor airPlus certification?

EPA Indoor airPlus is a federal certification for homes built with construction practices and materials that reduce indoor air pollutants, moisture, and mold risk — on top of standard ENERGY STAR requirements.
