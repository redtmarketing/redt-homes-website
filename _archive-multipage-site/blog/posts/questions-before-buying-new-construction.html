5 Questions to Ask Before Buying New Construction in Denver

By redT Homes Team · February 20, 2026

Not all new construction is built the same way. Before you sign anything, here are five questions worth asking any Denver builder — including a few most buyers don't think to ask until it's too late.

1. What's actually certified, versus just claimed?

"Energy efficient" and "green" are unregulated marketing terms. Ask specifically which third-party certifications the home carries — ENERGY STAR, EPA Indoor airPlus, WaterSense — and ask to see the documentation, not just the logo.

2. Is the home gas or all-electric?

This affects your monthly bills, your indoor air quality, and your maintenance costs for as long as you own the home. Ask what the heating and cooling system actually is, not just whether the kitchen has a gas hookup.

3. How is the price set relative to the neighborhood?

Some builders price to the maximum the lot allows. Ask how the home's price compares to the neighborhood median, and whether that's before or after typical upgrade costs.

4. What's the indoor air quality plan?

Ventilation, humidity control, and material selection all affect the air your family breathes daily. Ask whether the home meets EPA Indoor airPlus standards, which require documented moisture and ventilation practices beyond code minimums.

5. What happens after closing?

Ask about warranty coverage, who to call for post-move-in issues, and whether the builder has an ongoing relationship with the neighborhood, or has moved on by the time you close.
