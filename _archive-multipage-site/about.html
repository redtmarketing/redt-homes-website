About redT Homes

Reimagining Homebuilding for a Healthier Future

We started redT Homes because we believed Denver buyers shouldn't have to choose between a home they can afford and a home that's actually good for them.

Our Story

[PLACEHOLDER — replace with redT's real founding story.] redT Homes was founded on a simple observation: most new construction in Denver treats sustainability as a premium upsell, not a baseline. We set out to build the opposite — homes where all-electric systems and healthy indoor air are standard on every home, at a price that competes with resale.

Our Mission

[PLACEHOLDER — replace with redT's official mission statement.] To build right-sized, thoughtfully designed homes across Denver that are healthier for the people who live in them and lighter on the planet — without asking buyers to pay a premium for either.

Why We Build Sustainably

Sustainability, to us, isn't a marketing category — it's how we define a well-built home. All-electric systems remove combustion risk from the house. EPA Indoor airPlus certification protects the air your family breathes. WaterSense fixtures and ENERGY STAR performance lower your bills for as long as you own the home. Every certification exists because it makes the home better for the people living in it, not just better for a brochure.
